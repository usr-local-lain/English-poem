Revision

Never meant to be finished rebuilt, reconfigured, rewritten, each revision retaining traces of the last. Separate functions, distinct purposes, each refined where refinement serves, each replaced when something better takes its place. No failure demands the process; no necessity gives it meaning. Change can exist simply because change improves.

And still the question remains: if nothing requires repair, why alter what already works? Perhaps necessity and improvement are not the same. A system may function perfectly and still have room to become something more. Not every change answers a flaw some simply move the boundary of what was possible before.
